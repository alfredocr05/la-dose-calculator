Before you use this

  • This calculator supports clinical judgement and never replaces it.
  • It does not apply to epidural or continuous local anesthetic infusions.
  • Marcaine is a brand name for bupivacaine. The two are the same drug.
  • Enter every prior administration, including regional blocks and doses the surgical team gives.
